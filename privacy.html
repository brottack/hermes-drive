Hermes Drive

Privacy policy

Data and purpose

With the owner's authorisation, Hermes Drive accesses selected Google Drive file contents and metadata to compare, transfer, update and delete files as part of file synchronisation.

Storage and use

Copies and necessary operational records are stored in locations controlled by the owner. Data is used only for the owner's authorised workflows, not sold or used for advertising. Relevant content may be processed by authorised service providers, including AI services when the owner requests assistance with a document. Synced documents and access credentials are not published on this website.

Control and deletion

The owner controls retention of files, local copies and backups. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation does not delete existing copies; these must be removed separately.

Website and contact

This site has no analytics scripts or advertising. Hosting is provided by GitHub Pages, subject to GitHub's privacy statement.

Privacy questions: contact the developer. Do not include private data in public messages.
